# MOOTCOACH: ANALYSE EXPERIENCE

## 1. Goal
Design an advanced legal intelligence environment. The interface must visually communicate the AI's deep analysis process of the moot proposition.

## 2. Upload to Analysis Transition
- **Document Reveal:** When a proposition PDF is uploaded, don't just show a spinner. 
- **Scanning Effect:** Show the document being scanned. Use progressive highlighting of text.
- **Extraction Animation:** Visually pull "Facts", "Issues", and "Statutes" out of the document into a structured legal graph.

## 3. Visualizing the AI
- **Neural Connections:** Use subtle, flowing data lines connecting the uploaded document to extracted issues. 
- **Node Relationships:** Display issues and arguments as a graph or connected nodes, demonstrating how one legal precedent ties to a specific fact in the proposition.
- **Structured Legal Graph:** The UI should transition from a flat document view into a multi-panel data visualization view. 

## 4. Aesthetics
- **HUD Elements:** Use the Monospace typography token for data points, extraction confidence scores, and system logs.
- **Restraint:** Keep the animations smooth and purposeful. Do not clutter the screen; the extracted text must remain highly readable.
