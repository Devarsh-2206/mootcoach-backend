# MOOTCOACH: VISION

## 1. What is MootCoach?
MootCoach is an AI-powered moot court preparation platform. It helps law students prepare for moot court competitions by assisting with proposition analysis, legal issue identification, legal reasoning, argument structuring, memorial drafting, and AI-assisted oral-round practice.

## 2. Core Objective
The objective is to establish a distinctive visual language for a futuristic, premium, immersive legal-tech product. This is **not** a generic SaaS dashboard, a template, or a cyberpunk gaming website. It is a serious, next-generation legal-tech product.

## 3. The Target Emotional Response
When users interact with MootCoach, the target emotional response is:
> *"Wait. This is a moot court platform?"*

The experience should communicate that MootCoach is an advanced simulation environment combining AI intelligence, courtroom simulation, premium editorial design, and sophisticated data visualization.

## 4. Key Attributes
The MootCoach interface must feel:
- **Futuristic & High-Tech:** Advanced but subtle.
- **Cinematic & Immersive:** Like stepping into a simulation rather than a flat webpage.
- **Realistic & Legally Professional:** Rooted in the visual language of law firms and legal documents.
- **Premium & Sophisticated:** Expensive and intentional.
- **Smooth, Responsive, & Physically Interactive:** Using physics-based animations (springs, magnetic hover) that feel alive.

## 5. What MootCoach is NOT
- Not a generic AI SaaS product.
- Not a dashboard with random glowing borders or excessive neon.
- Not a simple CRUD application.
- Not overloaded with meaningless particle effects or "fake AI" terminology.
