# MOOTCOACH: LANDING PAGE

## 1. Structure
Avoid the generic SaaS structure (Hero -> 3 cards -> Features -> CTA). The landing page must be a storytelling experience that simulates entering the product.

### The Narrative Arc:
1. **The Courtroom (Hero):** Cinematic, immersive introduction.
2. **The Proposition:** Uploading the case.
3. **The Issues:** AI extracting legal points.
4. **The Arguments:** Structuring the case.
5. **The Bench:** The simulation experience.
6. **The Performance:** Moot strength scoring.
7. **MootCoach (CTA):** Enter the simulation.

## 2. The Opening Experience (Hero)
- **Concept:** A cinematic intro.
- **Sequence:**
  1. BLACK SCREEN.
  2. Subtle atmospheric movement (dust motes, dim light).
  3. A 3D courtroom environment slowly emerges from the shadows.
  4. Camera slowly pushes toward the judicial bench.
  5. Subtle legal metadata (green/gold text) fades in.
  6. **MOOTCOACH** logotype reveals via a cinematic mask.
  7. The environment blurs and transitions smoothly into the main UI.
- **Performance:** This must be an optimized video (WebM/MP4) or highly optimized WebGL. It must be short and immediately skippable for returning users.

## 3. Scroll Interactions
- **Scrolling is Navigating:** Scrolling should feel like diving deeper into the system.
- **Image Masking & Parallax:** As the user scrolls, feature images are revealed through expanding clip-paths while text rolls up sequentially.
- **Kinetic Typography:** Large, beautiful serif typography acting as background architectural elements.
