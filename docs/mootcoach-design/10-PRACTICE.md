# MOOTCOACH: PRACTICE / BENCH SIMULATOR

## 1. Goal
This is the flagship experience. It must be one of the most visually impressive parts of MootCoach. The student must feel: *"I am standing before a bench."*

## 2. Environment Design
- **Realism over Gamification:** This is not a video game. It is a serious simulation.
- **Dark Courtroom Aesthetic:** Deep shadows, warm directional lighting simulating courtroom lamps.
- **Subtle Depth:** The AI judge should feel physically present, represented either through a high-fidelity cinematic avatar (Higgsfield asset) or a highly sophisticated, abstract audio-reactive interface that feels physical (like glass and light).

## 3. Interface Elements
- **Microphone / Voice:** An elegant, physical-feeling microphone toggle. Audio waves should be sophisticated, perhaps resembling legal ink or glowing glass, not generic equalizer bars.
- **Timer:** Large, crisp Serif or Monospace timer tracking the oral round.
- **Transcript:** A scrolling, real-time transcription panel that looks like a deposition transcript.
- **Interrupts / Objections:** When the AI judge interrupts, the interface should respond immediately (e.g., the screen pulses subtly red, a glass panel slides in with the objection).
- **Speaking Indicator:** Clear distinction between when the user is speaking and when the bench is speaking.

## 4. Camera & Transition
- Entering the simulator should feel like a camera pushing through doors into a dark room.
- Exiting should pull back, returning to the bright/analytical workspace.
