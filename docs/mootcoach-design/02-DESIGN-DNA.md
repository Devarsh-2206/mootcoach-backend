# MOOTCOACH: DESIGN DNA

## 1. Foundation: Dark Cinematic
- **Palette:** Deep black, near-black charcoal, controlled contrast.
- **Lighting:** Subtle atmospheric gradients, cinematic lighting, restrained highlights.
- **Depth:** Elements should sit in a spatial environment, not just on a flat background.

## 2. Brand Identity: Modern Legal / Editorial
- **Inspiration:** Legal documents, editorial publications, premium law firms, courtroom architecture.
- **Precision:** Institutional precision modernized with technical intelligence.

## 3. Surfaces: Restrained Glass / Translucency
- **Usage:** Translucent panels, subtle blur (backdrop-filter), layered surfaces.
- **Restraint:** Do NOT make every element glass. Glass should feel like a specific material in the environment, allowing environmental visibility and controlled reflections.

## 4. Spatial Design
- **Environment:** The interface should sometimes feel like a 3D environment.
- **Techniques:** Depth, perspective, layered elements, environmental backgrounds, subtle 3D spatial transitions.

## 5. AI Visual Language
- **Purposeful Visualization:** Neural networks, connected nodes, flowing information, and structured legal graphs should communicate *actual product functionality*.
- **No Decoration:** Avoid meaningless AI decorations (e.g., random particle backgrounds).

## 6. Technical Language
- **HUD Details:** Subtle HUD-like details such as small labels, metadata, technical indicators, system status, micro typography, and thin lines.
- **Subtlety:** Keep this restrained. MootCoach is a legal tool, not a sci-fi command center.
