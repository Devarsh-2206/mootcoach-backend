About MootCoach

MootCoach helps law students prepare for moot court. You upload the moot proposition, and MootCoach works through the case with you: what the court will care about, how to argue each side, and how your arguments hold up when a judge pushes back.

What it does

  • Reads the proposition. It identifies the court or tribunal, the governing law and the issues, so the advice fits the forum: a Supreme Court writ, a civil trial, an arbitration or an international court.
  • Helps you draft. It plans and drafts memorial arguments with you, and checks the cases it suggests against published sources instead of taking the AI’s word for them.
  • Puts you in front of a bench. You argue by text or by voice before an AI judge that questions your actual submissions, then you get a scored report of the round.
  • Keeps track of the season. A calendar of upcoming moots with deadlines and a preparation plan.

Why we built it

Mooting is one of the best ways to learn to think and argue like a lawyer, but good coaching is scarce. Most students get little feedback before a competition, and general-purpose AI tools invent case law and ignore which court the case is in. MootCoach is built to be careful about both.
