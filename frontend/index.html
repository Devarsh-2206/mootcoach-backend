Constitution of the bench

Three temperaments. One is waiting for you.

T

The Textualist

Precise · Provision-bound

Wants the section, the sub-clause, the exact words.

"Counsel, take me to the provision. Which limb are you actually invoking?"
I

The Interventionist

Relentless · Interrupting

You won't finish a sentence unchallenged.

"Stop there. If I'm against you, what is your second-best argument?"
S

The Silent Bench

Sparse · Unreadable

No questions, no signals — you carry the round alone.

"…Proceed, counsel."
