How MootCoach Works

Three steps from raw proposition to confident advocate.

01

Create Moot Workspace

Upload your moot proposition PDF and set up your workspace. The AI reads it and starts building context immediately.

02

Practice with AI

Enter the AI Practice Room. Argue your side, face dynamic bench questions, and iterate on your oral submissions in real time.

03

Receive Strategic Feedback

Get structured critique on argument clarity, counter-argument readiness, precedent use, and oral advocacy — every round.

Capabilities

Engineered for Outcomes

Not features. Results you can carry into the moot hall.

⚖

Identify Legal Issues Instantly

AI parses your proposition and surfaces every contestable issue, relevant precedent, and constitutional angle before you start drafting.

◎

Improve Oral Advocacy with Realtime Critique

Submit your speech, receive scored feedback on structure, language, and legal precision. Know exactly where you lose the bench.

✦

Build Unassailable Arguments

Construct arguments with proper ratio, precedent chains, and pre-emptive rebuttals — structured for competition judges.

◈

Memorial Guidance That Wins Marks

AI-assisted feedback on your written memorial — structure, research depth, citation style, and persuasive framing.

↯

Anticipate Every Bench Question

Train against an AI bench that targets your weakest arguments. Walk into the moot hall with answers already prepared.

▣

Track Progress Round by Round

Analytics across all your sessions. Watch scores improve, spot recurring weaknesses, and measure growth over time.
