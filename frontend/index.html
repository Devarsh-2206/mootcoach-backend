Process

How MootCoach Works

Three steps from raw proposition to confident advocate.

01

Create Moot Workspace

Upload your moot proposition PDF and set up your workspace. The AI reads it and starts building context immediately.

02

Practice with AI

Enter the AI Practice Room. Argue your side, face dynamic bench questions, and iterate on your oral submissions in real time.

03

Receive Strategic Feedback

Get structured critique on argument clarity, counter-argument readiness, precedent use, and oral advocacy — every round.

Capabilities

Engineered for Outcomes

⚖

Identify Legal Issues Instantly

AI parses your proposition and surfaces every contestable issue, relevant precedent, and constitutional angle before you start drafting.
