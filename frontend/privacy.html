Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Confidentiality

Moot propositions and memorial strategies are often confidential until a competition is over. Your uploads are processed for analysis and saved only to your own signed-in account. Other users cannot see them.

2. Information we collect

We collect the account information needed to run your workspace: your name, email address and, if you give it, your law school. We store the propositions you upload, the analyses, drafts and practice-round results you create, so that you can return to them from any device.

3. Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We use them only to create and identify your MootCoach account and to show your name in the app. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell or share them.

4. AI processing and other services

To analyse a proposition or draft, MootCoach sends its text to our AI providers, Groq and Google (Gemini), for processing. Your account and saved work are stored with Google Firebase. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What those providers may retain is governed by their own terms and depends on the plan MootCoach runs on. If your proposition is under embargo, treat uploading it as you would sending it to any service outside your team, and check your competition’s rules first.

5. Your control over your data

You can clear your workspace at any time. To have your account and all of its data deleted, email us at the address below and we will do it.
