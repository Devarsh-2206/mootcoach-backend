Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Confidentiality

Moot propositions and memorial strategies are often confidential until a competition is over. Your uploads are processed for analysis and saved only to your own signed-in account. Other users cannot see them.

2. Information we collect

We collect the account information needed to run your workspace: your name, email address and, if you give it, your law school. We store the propositions you upload, the analyses, drafts and practice-round results you create, so that you can return to them from any device.

3. Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We use them only to create and identify your MootCoach account and to show your name in the app. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell or share them.

4. The services that handle your data

MootCoach is run on a small number of outside services. Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Google (Gemini, through Vertex AI and the Gemini API) and Groq: the text of your propositions, drafts and practice rounds, to produce analyses, drafts, questions and scores. In a voice round, your voice is streamed to Google to run the judge. MootCoach keeps the written transcript and the score, not the audio.
  • Indian Kanoon: the names and citations of the cases MootCoach suggests, to confirm each one exists. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Google Firebase: your account and everything you save in your workspace.
  • Render: hosts the MootCoach website and server, which pass your requests on to the services above.
  • Resend: sends MootCoach's emails (welcome, pilot updates, password resets). It receives your email address and the message.
  • Zoho Mail: our inbox. If you email us, your message is stored there.
  • Microsoft Clarity: shows us how the website is used (clicks, scrolling, where people get stuck) so we can fix confusing screens. The contents of your workspace (propositions, analyses, drafts and transcripts) are hidden from it, and we do not send it your name or email address.

These services may process data outside India. What each may retain is governed by its own terms. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than these services.

If your proposition is under embargo, treat uploading it as you would sending it to any service outside your team, and check your competition’s rules first.

5. Emails

We email you about your account (password resets and confirming your address) and, during the pilot, a few short emails about your place and your feedback. Every reminder email has a link to stop reminders.

6. Cookies and storage in your browser

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to remember small preferences, such as light or dark mode. Microsoft Clarity sets its own cookies to measure usage. We use no advertising cookies.

7. How long we keep your data, and your control over it

Your account and saved work are kept until you delete them or ask us to. You can clear your workspace at any time. To have your account and all of its data deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email us at the address below and we will do it. Pilot feedback is kept with your name and email so we can follow up on it; ask and we will delete yours too.

8. Contact and complaints

Questions or complaints about this policy or your data: support@mootcoach.in. We aim to reply within a few working days.
